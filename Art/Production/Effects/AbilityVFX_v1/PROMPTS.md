# Deepcaller ability VFX v1

Generated with Codex's built-in image generation mode as top-down,
transparent-runtime RimWorld assets. Each sheet was generated against a flat
`#00ff00` chroma-key field, then keyed locally with the imagegen skill's
soft-matte/despill helper. Runtime frames are rebuilt by
`build_runtime_assets.sh`.

## Shared direction

Cute cosmic-ocean horror for Deepcaller: glossy midnight-navy flesh, cyan
bioluminescence, small violet accents, clean readable silhouette, painterly
cartoon game sprite, orthographic top-down/three-quarter view, no text, no
border, no cast shadow, and no green inside the subject.

## Riptide

A precise 2x2 animation atlas of one magical ocean vortex: (1) tight awakening
whirlpool, (2) expanding spiral, (3) broad full-force current, (4) collapsing
hollow ring. The vortex should feel like luminous deep water and psychic
current, energetic rather than photorealistic, centered with generous padding,
consistent perspective and color, four equal cells, flat chroma background.

## Grasp

A precise 3x2 atlas: four chronological frames of the same kawaii abyssal
tentacle erupting from a small ground portal and curling tighter; one isolated
vertical cyan wavy energy tether; one release splash with a retreating
tentacle-tip. Constant tentacle width and portal footprint across the four
animation frames, readable sucker details, no victim included, six equal
cells, flat chroma background.

## Ink Veil

A precise 3x2 atlas: four chronological expressions of the same compact
three-eyed living abyssal ink cloud, plus one large eyeless cloud puff and one
small flat eyeless puff. Soft billowing lobes, glossy navy ink, cyan eyes and
bioluminescent edge droplets, mischievous rather than frightening, constant
core size across animation frames, six equal cells, flat chroma background.

## Tideguard

A precise 2x2 animation atlas of one empty-centered living-water shield ring:
(1) calm circular current, (2) asymmetric surge, (3) bright compressed impact,
(4) destabilizing splash-ring. Thin cyan-white water and psychic light with
small violet accents; the transparent center must remain open so the protected
creature stays visible; consistent outer diameter, four equal cells, flat
chroma background.

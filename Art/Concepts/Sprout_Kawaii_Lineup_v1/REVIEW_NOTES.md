# Deepcaller Sprout — kawaii-horror lineup v1

These are exploratory south-facing concepts. They do not replace runtime art.
Every candidate uses a terrain-neutral black psychic rift with a cyan-violet
edge; the creature design is the variable.

## Candidates

- **A — Pebble:** short, round baseline with a perky curl.
- **B — Ribbon:** slender, lively S-curve.
- **C — Dumpling:** squat, plush little blob.
- **D — Bud:** folded tip protecting a psychic glow.
- **E — Spiral:** playful nautilus curl.
- **F — Plush:** broad, graphic, floppy silhouette.
- **G — Freckled:** abstract luminous constellation.
- **H — Shy:** inward curl hiding its suckers.
- **I — Excited:** upright spring with bright ring markings.
- **J — Sleepy:** relaxed tentacle draped over the rift.

## Review questions

Score each candidate from 1–5 for:

1. Immediate cuteness.
2. Recognizable tentacle silhouette.
3. Horror-theme presence without gore.
4. Fit with the magical rift.
5. Readability at RimWorld scale.
6. Desire to summon and keep one around.

Select two or three finalists for full south/east/north idle and recoil sheets.
